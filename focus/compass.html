The 5 rules for everyone

1Smile and use their name twice.
2Listen more than you talk.
3Remember one detail and ask about it next time.
4Make small promises and keep every one.
5End on a high: the last minute is what they remember.

When you're unsure, go one level more formal. You can always warm up later, but it's hard to take back oversharing.
